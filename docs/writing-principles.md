# Writing Principles

## The standard

This project treats technical writing as a craft, not a formatting exercise.

Good technical writing should:

- identify a real problem
- explain design value
- reflect judgment
- stay readable
- avoid inflation
- remain credible under promotional pressure

## Key principles

### 1. Start from reality
Anchor the writing in a real problem, friction point, or engineering context.

### 2. Explain value
Do not stop at features. Explain why a feature matters.

### 3. Show judgment
Good writing includes informed preference and reasoning.

### 4. Respect trade-offs
Engineering writing improves when it explains why one path was chosen over another.

### 5. Preserve proportion
Do not overstate. Understatement is often stronger.
